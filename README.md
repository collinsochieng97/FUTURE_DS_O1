Business Sales Performance Analytics
 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing business sales data to uncover valuable insights that can support strategic decision-making. Using Python for data analysis and Power BI for visualization, the project identifies revenue trends, top-performing products, customer behavior patterns, and geographical sales performance.
The objective is to transform raw sales data into actionable business intelligence through data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and interactive dashboards.
________________________________________
 Objectives
Analyze overall business sales performance.
Identify top-performing products by revenue.
Discover high-value customers.
Examine sales trends over time.
Compare revenue performance across countries.
Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for decision-making.

UnitPrice	Price per unit
CustomerID	Customer identifier
Country	Customer country
Revenue	Calculated sales revenue
________________________________________
 Key Findings
Revenue Performance
Total revenue generated exceeded 10.67 million.
Sales increased steadily throughout the year.
Peak revenue was recorded during the final quarter, indicating strong seasonal demand.
Product Analysis
A small number of products contributed significantly to overall revenue.
Top-selling products accounted for a substantial portion of business growth.
Customer Insights
High-value customers generated a large share of total revenue.
Customer purchasing patterns revealed opportunities for retention and loyalty programs.
Geographic Performance
Revenue was concentrated in a few key countries.
Certain regions consistently outperformed others, indicating strong market opportunities.
